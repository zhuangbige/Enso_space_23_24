## Method Details To Preserve

- The paper compares 2023/24 against 1982/83, 1997/98, and 2015/16.
- Many anomalies are relative to 1982-2022.
- Figure 1 uses June-January averages and compares Nino-3 SST with central-western
  Pacific 10 m zonal wind and SOI.
- Extended Data Figure 2 separates June-December 2023 SST anomalies into
  detrended and background-trend components for 1982-2023.
- The AGCM analysis uses five CAM6 experiments: global observed SST, Pacific-only
  anomalies, non-Pacific anomalies, background-trend-related forcing, and
  detrended/event-related forcing.
- The wind-stress-prescribed CGCM uses GFDL CM2.1 with daily ERA5 wind stress,
  initialized from 1941 and analysed over 1982-2023. InitApr2023 removes
  post-1-April 31-day running-mean wind stress anomalies while retaining
  sub-31-day variability.
