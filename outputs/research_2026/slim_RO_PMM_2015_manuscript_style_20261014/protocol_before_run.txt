2015 sLIM RO + standard PMM; manuscript-style presentation

Scope: Reuse the audited seasonal RO estimator and add standard PMM states.
Main PMM choice fixed before this rerun: the canonical MCA SST coefficient,
following the index provider's guidance that SST better represents the coupled
mode. Retain canonical wind and SST+wind variants; do not select by 2015 skill.

Three separate fitting protocols:
1. paper_2002_2024: exactly reuse the mean-removed, detrended T/h series from
   the Fig.3a/Fig.4 reconstruction, Jan 2002-Apr 2025; fit Jan 2002-Dec 2024.
   This includes 2015 and is a retrospective reconstruction/extension.
2. past_2002_2014: fit all preprocessing and coefficients on Jan 2002-Dec 2014;
   same start year as the paper. Primary event-excluded hindcast.
3. past_1982_2014: same procedure with a longer historical record, kept as
   a training-length sensitivity, not chosen to maximize 2015 skill.

All protocols use ERSSTv5 Niño3.4 and GODAS equatorial area-mean D20 (h, m).
PMM: cached author-provided standard monthly MCA SST and Wind indices; the
provider fixes patterns over 1950-2005. Center using the training period,
then normalize each state by training SD. No extra ENSO regression or smoothing.
The current index/reanalysis vintage makes past-only experiments retrospective
hindcasts; the provider's original historical preprocessing cannot be controlled.

Estimator unchanged: paper_ro_slim.fit, annual + semiannual harmonics,
second-order seasonal perturbation solution, linear six-step-per-month
interpolation. Refit the complete joint operator for every state selection.
RO+PMM is a new extension here, not an author-reported result.

Initial states: observed monthly means for February-June 2015. Forecast leads
1-9 months; lead 0 displayed only as initialization and excluded from scoring.
RK4 with 24 steps/month, deterministic, all states including PMM freely evolve
after initialization. No future observed PMM forcing or trajectory resetting.
Five initializations x nine targets = 45 paired forecasts per variable/model.
Month labels refer to monthly-state initialization, not the IRI issue month.
No smoothing of observations or predictions to improve visual agreement.

Primary figures: Fig.3-style two phase panels above time-series and skill panels;
black observations, coral El Niño forecasts, framed axes and inward ticks.
RO vs RO+PMM-SST. A Fig.4-style plume supplement shows T and h separately;
an ablation figure retains wind and joint PMM. Each protocol is separately labeled.
Phase axes use the same RO training SD for all models in a protocol; all models
share the same T/h initial conditions and preprocessing. Within-model timeseries
show detrended anomaly units. Axis ranges shared across protocol comparison pages.

Metrics: correlation and RMSE for T and h, matched initializations and targets;
per-start metrics; normalized joint T/h RMSE; RMSE percent change relative to RO.
These 45 pairs are overlapping forecasts of one event, not 45 independent events.
No significance interval or claim of general PMM improvement from 2015 alone.
Same-coefficient PMM-to-T/h cuts retained to distinguish removing a coupling
from refitting the two-state model.
